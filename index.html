I started learning programming because I wanted to create my own games and build artificial intelligence systems inspired by JARVIS. What began as curiosity about what technology could create eventually became a genuine interest in programming and software development.

Starting with Fundamentals

I began building my programming fundamentals through YouTube tutorials and structured learning resources such as freeCodeCamp. These resources helped me understand the basics of programming and gave me a foundation to start applying what I learned through practice.

Transition to Web Development

My transition into web development began when I was tasked with creating a website for our school as part of our Grade 12 research and capstone project. This experience gave me the opportunity to apply what I had learned about programming to a real-world project and sparked my interest in web development.
